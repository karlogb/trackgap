How it works

Install it, connect GA4, done. Nothing goes into your theme.

STEP 01

Reads your orders

Straight from Shopify, server-side. Adblockers and consent banners cannot touch this one.

STEP 02

Checks what arrived

Pulls the transactions GA4 actually recorded and matches them by order ID, one at a time.

STEP 03

Works out why

Broken tag, declined consent, duplicate events, or a value sent without shipping. These are very different problems.

STEP 04

Sends them back

Where there is consent and an identifier, the missing conversion goes back to GA4 with its original source. Where there is not, we say so instead.

Why this exists

I run measurement for around eighty e-commerce stores. The same thing kept happening: a client's numbers stopped matching, and finding out why meant exporting orders, pulling GA4 transactions and diffing them in a spreadsheet - every time, by hand.

Shopify tracking gaps, explained

The questions merchants ask before installing.

Why do Shopify orders and GA4 transactions not match?

Common causes include declined analytics consent, blocked or broken purchase tags, duplicate events, processing delays, and order values sent without shipping or tax. TrackGap matches orders by ID so these causes can be separated.

Does TrackGap modify my Shopify theme?

No. TrackGap uses official Shopify APIs and a Shopify web pixel extension. There is no theme code for the merchant to install or maintain.

Can TrackGap recover missing GA4 conversions?

Yes, when the visitor granted the required consent and a valid GA4 client identifier is available. TrackGap does not send conversions when consent is declined or cannot be demonstrated.

What customer data does TrackGap store?

TrackGap does not request customer names, email addresses, postal addresses, phone numbers, or payment details. It uses order IDs, values, timestamps, traffic sources, and pseudonymous analytics identifiers needed for reconciliation.
